Vampire Diaries

The lives, loves, dangers and disasters in the town, Mystic Falls, Virginia. Creatures of unspeakable horror lurk beneath this town as a teenage girl is suddenly torn between two vampire brothers.

Starring: Nina Dobrev, Paul Wesley, Ian Somerhalder, Steven R. McQueen, Sara Canning, Kat Graham, Candice King, Zach Roerig, Michael Trevino, Matthew Davis, Joseph Morgan

Directors: Julie Plec, Kevin Williamson

Rating: 80% Liked

Genres: Fantasy, Based on Books, Teen, Dramas

1. Pilot

Reeling from the death of her parents, 17-year-old Elena becomes interested in a handsome new student named Stefan, who's hiding a dark secret

2. The Night of the Comet

Elena goes to the Salvatore house to talk to Stefan but finds Damon there instead. Damon reveals surprising information about Stefan's past.

3. Friday Night Bites

Elena invites Stefan and Bonnie to dinner, hoping the two will bond, but the evening is disrupted by the unexpected arrival of Damon and Caroline.

4. Family Ties

Stefan escorts Elena to the town's Founder's Party. Damon tells Elena a story about the Salvatores, leaving her with questions Stefan won't answer.

5. You're Undead to Me

Stefan is hopeful about his plan to get rid of Damon and decides to reveal parts of his past to Elena. Bonnie seeks advice from her grandmother.

6. Lost Girls

Stefan reveals to Elena the Salvatore family history and how his rivalry with Damon began. In the present, Damon takes control of Vicki's future.

7. Haunted

Bonnie's abilities surprise Damon. Elena asks Jeremy not to see Vicki. Matt takes Vicki to a haunted house, but the night takes a terrifying turn.

8. 162 Candles

Stefan gets a surprise birthday visit from old friend Lexi. Caroline tries to get Damon's medallion back from Bonnie.

9. History Repeating

Jeremy gets a break from the mysterious new history teacher, Alaric Saltzman. Damon tells Stefan the stunning reason he returned to Mystic Falls.

10. The Turning Point

When Sheriff Forbes tells Damon about another attack, he offers to track down the killer, learning startling information about the town's founders.

11. Bloodlines

Damon goes to Georgia to surprise an old flame, Bree, and enlist her help. Meanwhile, Stefan uncovers a startling clue to the past.
